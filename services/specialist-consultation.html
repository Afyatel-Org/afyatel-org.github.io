How it works

From tap to care.

  1. Choose a specialty

    Start from the specialty list rather than a name.

  2. Compare specialists

    See experience, languages, facility, distance and fee.

  3. Book and pay

    Chat, in-clinic or home, paid by M-Pesa or billed to your cover.

  4. Keep the record

    Everything the specialist writes stays in your account.

Who provides it

Licensed, and checked.

Specialists registered with the Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council, and allied professionals verified against their own boards.

  • Good to know

    Which specialties have providers near you depends on who has joined in your area.

  • Good to know

    Each provider sets their own consultation fee, shown before you book.
