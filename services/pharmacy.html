Pharmacy

Order from registered pharmacies near you, attach the prescription your doctor wrote, and have it delivered to the exact spot you pin.

What is included

What you can do here.

Order over-the-counter items

Browse a pharmacy’s shelf and add what you need.

Send a prescription

Attach the latest prescription from your consultation notes, or upload a photo or file.

Prescription-only orders

Send just the prescription and let the pharmacist price it and confirm with you.

Refills

Reorder the medicines you take regularly.

Delivery to your pin

Drop a pin so the rider can find you without calling.

How it works

From tap to care.

  1. Pick a pharmacy

    Registered pharmacies, nearest first.

  2. Add items or a prescription

    From your doctor’s notes, a photo or a file.

  3. Pin the delivery point

    Address plus a map pin.

  4. The pharmacy confirms

    It prices, confirms with you and arranges delivery.

Who provides it

Licensed, and checked.

Pharmacists and pharmacies registered with the Pharmacy and Poisons Board.

  • Good to know

    Prescription-only medicines are supplied only against a valid prescription.

  • Good to know

    Stock and prices are set by each pharmacy.
