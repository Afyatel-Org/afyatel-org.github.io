Services

Ten services, one app.

Everything Afyatel offers, grouped the way the app shows it. Open any service to see what is included, how it works and who provides it.

General consultation

See a general practitioner, family physician, clinical officer or nurse for everyday health concerns, by secure chat, at their clinic or at your home.

6 things you can do

Specialist consultation

Browse specialists by field and book the one you need, without waiting for a referral letter to find out who is available.

4 things you can do

Pharmacy

Order from registered pharmacies near you, attach the prescription your doctor wrote, and have it delivered to the exact spot you pin.

5 things you can do

Laboratory and imaging

Choose from a standard list of more than 1,700 tests, compare what laboratories charge, have the sample collected where you are, and read the results in the app.

5 things you can do

Dental

Find dentists and orthodontists near you and book a visit at their clinic.

4 things you can do

Gynae and women’s health

Book gynaecologists, obstetricians and midwives for consultations and antenatal care, in private and at your own pace.

4 things you can do

Home care

Ask a nurse or clinician to come to you.

4 things you can do

Wellness communities

Join peer support groups for the conditions and goals that matter to you.

4 things you can do

Emergency and ambulance

In an emergency, call 999 or 112 first.

3 things you can do

Medical-legal

Find providers who complete P3 forms, write medical reports and give expert opinion.
