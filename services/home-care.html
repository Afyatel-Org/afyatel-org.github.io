Nurses and clinicians at your door

Home care.

Ask a nurse or clinician to come to you. Pin your home on the map and they get directions to the door.

Watch the stepsGet the app
What is included

What you can do here.

Nursing visits

A registered nurse at home for checks and care.

Clinician visits

A doctor or clinical officer who consults at home.

Care for an older relative

Book on behalf of a family member, at their address.

Follow-up after hospital

Checks and reviews at home once you are discharged.

How it works

From tap to care.

  1. Search Home care

    Providers who visit, nearest first.

  2. Choose Home as the mode

    Pick a time that suits the household.

  3. Pin your location

    Address plus a map pin for directions.

  4. Receive the visit

    Notes are saved to your record afterwards.

Who provides it

Licensed, and checked.

Nurses registered with the Nursing Council of Kenya, clinical officers and doctors.

  • Good to know

    Your pin is shared with the provider you booked, for that visit.

  • Good to know

    Home-visit fees are set by each provider and shown before you book.
