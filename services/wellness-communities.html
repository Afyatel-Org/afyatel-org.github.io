How it works

From tap to care.

  1. Open Wellness

    Browse the groups available.

  2. Join a group

    Some groups are free; some charge a membership fee, shown before you pay.

  3. Take part

    Read, ask and share at your own pace.

Who provides it

Licensed, and checked.

Groups are moderated. Posts that give harmful advice or share private information are removed.

  • Good to know

    Community posts and group chats are for support and general information. They are not medical advice for your situation.
