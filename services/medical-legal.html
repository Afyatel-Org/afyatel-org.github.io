What is included

What you can do here.

P3 forms

Medical examination forms completed by a qualified provider.

Medical reports

Reports for employers, insurers, schools or courts.

Expert opinion

A professional opinion on a medical question.

How it works

From tap to care.

  1. Search Medical-legal

    Providers who offer this service.

  2. Book a consultation

    Most reports need an in-person examination.

  3. Receive the document

    The provider issues the form or report.

Who provides it

Licensed, and checked.

Doctors registered with the Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council.

  • Good to know

    Requirements differ by form and institution. Confirm what you need with the provider before you attend.
