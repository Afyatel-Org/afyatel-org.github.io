How it works

From tap to care.

  1. Search Dental

    Dentists and orthodontists, nearest first.

  2. Pick a clinic and time

    In-clinic visits are booked by slot.

  3. Pay or bill your cover

    M-Pesa, or SHA or your insurer.

  4. Attend

    Directions to the clinic are in the booking.

Who provides it

Licensed, and checked.

Dentists registered with the Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council.

  • Good to know

    Most dental care needs an in-person visit.

  • Good to know

    Each provider sets their own fees, shown before you book.
