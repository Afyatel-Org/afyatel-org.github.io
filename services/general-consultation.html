GPs and family physicians

General consultation.

See a general practitioner, family physician, clinical officer or nurse for everyday health concerns, by secure chat, at their clinic or at your home.

Watch the stepsGet the app
What is included

What you can do here.

New symptoms

Fever, cough, stomach upsets, aches, rashes and the other reasons people usually see a doctor.

Follow-up visits

Review how you are doing after an earlier consultation, with your notes already in front of the provider.

Long-term conditions

Regular check-ins for conditions such as hypertension and diabetes, alongside your medication record.

Prescriptions

A prescription written into your consultation notes, ready to send to a pharmacy.

Lab and imaging orders

Tests ordered for you by name, which you can book with a laboratory in the app.

Care for your child

A parent or guardian can seek care for a child through their own account.

How it works

From tap to care.

  1. Find a provider

    Search by name, or list verified providers nearest first within 5, 10 or 25 km.

  2. Book a time

    Choose chat, an in-clinic visit or a home visit, and pick a slot.

  3. Pay or bill your cover

    Pay with M-Pesa, or ask for the visit to be billed to SHA or your insurer.

  4. Consult and follow up

    Notes, prescriptions and lab orders are saved under Activity.

Who provides it

Licensed, and checked.

Doctors (GP), clinical officers and nurses, each verified against their regulator.

  • Good to know

    Each provider sets their own consultation fee, shown before you book.

  • Good to know

    Remote consultations have limits: a provider cannot examine you physically and may ask you to attend in person.

  • Good to know

    Video consultations are planned and not yet available.
