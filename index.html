Home care

The clinic that comes home.

Not every visit should mean a matatu ride. Ask a nurse or clinician to come to you, and pin the exact gate.

  • Book a nurse, clinical officer or doctor who does home visits
  • Drop a map pin so they get directions to your door
  • Notes from the visit are saved to your record

How it works

Three steps, no waiting room.

  1. Find a verified provider

    Search by service or specialty, or let the map show who is close and available now.

  2. Book and consult

    Choose chat, a clinic visit or a home visit. Your provider sees the health record you share.

  3. Pay and follow up

    Pay with M-Pesa, or have the visit billed to SHA or your insurer. Notes, prescriptions and results stay in your account.

  1. Register with your licence

    Choose your cadre and give your licence number. It is checked against your regulator’s register.

  2. Go online

    Set your fee, availability and specialties. Patients near you can now find and book you.

  3. Care, record, get paid

    Chat, write notes, prescribe and order tests. Every payment is itemised under Earnings.

Privacy and safety

Your record is yours.

Health data is the most sensitive data there is. Afyatel handles it under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Privacy PolicyTerms of UseDelete your account

  • Never sold, never used for advertising

    We do not sell personal data, and health information is never used to choose the ads you see.

  • No third-party trackers

    The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising trackers, and never reads your contacts, messages or call logs.

  • Shared with the providers you choose

    Your record goes to the providers you book, message or pay, so that they can care for you.

  • Leave whenever you like

    Delete your account from inside the app, or by email if you no longer have it installed.

Visit the help centre

What is Afyatel?

Afyatel is a mobile app that connects people in Kenya with licensed doctors, clinical officers, nurses, pharmacies, laboratories and ambulance services. You can find care near you, consult, order medicine, book tests and keep your health record in one place.

How do I know a provider is genuine?

Every provider gives a licence number at sign-up, which is checked against their regulator before they can see patients. Verified providers carry a tick.

How much does a consultation cost?

Each provider sets their own fee, and you see it before you book. Downloading and creating an account costs nothing.

Can I use SHA or insurance?

You can ask for a booking or order to be billed to SHA or your insurer by giving the scheme and your member number. The provider bills the scheme and confirms your cover; you remain responsible for anything the scheme does not pay.

Who can see my health record?

You, the providers you book, message or pay, and authorised Afyatel staff. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
