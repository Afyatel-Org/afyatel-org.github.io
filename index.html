What is inside

Care that comes to you.

One account for the whole journey, from the first question to the follow-up.

Consultations

Book a general practitioner, clinical officer, nurse or specialist. Consult by secure chat, at their clinic, or ask for a home visit.

Pharmacy and refills

Order from registered pharmacies. Attach the prescription from your doctor's notes or upload one, then pin where it should be delivered.

Lab tests and imaging

Choose from a standard list of tests, arrange sample collection, and receive your results in the app, shared with the doctor who ordered them.

Your health record

Vitals, BMI, allergies, conditions and medicines in one place, with reminders for each dose and a clear picture of how well you are keeping to them.

Community and wellness

Follow health updates and stories, and join wellness groups for the conditions that matter to you. For support and general information, never a substitute for your own consultation.

Care near you

See verified providers on a map, nearest first, within the distance you choose. A shortcut to emergency numbers and ambulance services is always one tap away.

How it works

Three steps, no waiting room.

  1. Find a verified provider

    Search by service or specialty, or let the map show who is close and available now.

  2. Book and consult

    Choose chat, a clinic visit or a home visit. Your provider sees the health record you choose to share.

  3. Pay and follow up

    Pay with M-Pesa, or ask for the visit to be billed to SHA or your insurer. Notes, prescriptions and results stay in your account.

Installing the pilot build

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and download the file.
  2. Open the download. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open Afyatel and create your account with your email address.

The pilot build is for testing with invited users. When Afyatel is released on Google Play this page will link to the store, and the pilot build will be retired in favour of the store version.

Privacy and safety

Your record is yours.

Health data is the most sensitive data there is. Afyatel handles it under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Privacy PolicyTerms of UseDelete your account

  • Never sold, never used for advertising

    We do not sell personal data, and health information is never used to choose the ads you see.

  • No third-party trackers

    The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising trackers, and never reads your contacts, messages or call logs.

  • Shared with the providers you choose

    Your record goes to the providers you book, message or pay, so that they can care for you.

  • Leave whenever you like

    Delete your account from inside the app, or by email if you no longer have it installed.

Afyatel connects you with independent licensed providers. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional. Afyatel is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 999 or 112.
