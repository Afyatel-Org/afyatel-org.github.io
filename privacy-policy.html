Afyatel Privacy Policy

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE] · Version 1.0

This policy explains how Lion Shop Enterprises ("Afyatel", "we") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the Afyatel mobile app. We handle your data under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and its regulations. Health data is "sensitive personal data" under that Act, and we treat it with the highest level of care.

In short: we use your information to connect you with healthcare providers and to run the services you ask for. We never sell your data and never use your health information for advertising.

1. Who we are

The data controller is Lion Shop Enterprises, [Physical address, Nairobi, Kenya], registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) under registration number [ODPC REG. NO.]. Contact our data protection officer at [privacy@yourdomain].

Healthcare providers on Afyatel (doctors, clinical officers, nurses, pharmacists, laboratories) are independent licensed professionals. When they create clinical records about you (consultation notes, prescriptions, lab results) they are also responsible for those records under their professional duty of confidentiality.

2. Who can use Afyatel

Afyatel is for people aged 18 and over. A parent or guardian may use their own account to seek care for a child; that information is held on the parent's account. If we learn we have collected data from someone under 18 without a guardian, we will delete it.

6. Transfers outside Kenya

Our main database is hosted by Supabase in the United States, and some other service providers also process data outside Kenya (see the table above). We only transfer data where the Data Protection Act allows it: with appropriate safeguards in our contracts, and, for health data, with your explicit consent, which you give at sign-up. You can ask us for details of the safeguards.

Because Afyatel supports health care for people in Kenya, we also keep an accurate, up-to-date copy of your data in a data centre in Kenya ([Kenyan data centre provider]), as the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021 require.

8. How we protect it

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our hosting provider.
  • Row-level security rules in our database make sure a patient's record is visible only to them, to providers they have booked or messaged, and to authorised Afyatel staff.
  • Staff access is limited to what their role needs, and is logged.
  • Payment PINs are entered only on your phone's M-Pesa prompt; we never see or store them.
  • If a breach puts your data at risk, we will notify the ODPC within 72 hours and tell you without delay, as the Act requires.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used (this policy);
  • access your personal data and get a copy of it;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data (subject to the retention rules in section 7);
  • object to processing, or withdraw your consent;
  • receive your data in a portable format (data portability);
  • complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (www.odpc.go.ke).

To use these rights, email [privacy@yourdomain] or use the in-app options. We reply within 14 days (Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021). To delete your account, go to Profile → Privacy & legal → Delete account, or use the form at [DELETE ACCOUNT URL].

10. Permissions on your phone

  • Location (only while the app is open): to show providers near you, and for providers to pin their facility. You can say no and still search by name.
  • Photos and files: only the items you pick, to share with your provider or post.
  • Notifications: appointment, chat and medication reminders. You can turn them off in Settings.

11. Medical disclaimer

Afyatel is a platform that connects you with independent licensed providers. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat. It is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 999 or 112.
