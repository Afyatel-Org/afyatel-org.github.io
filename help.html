About Afyatel

What is Afyatel?

Afyatel is a mobile app that connects people in Kenya with licensed doctors, clinical officers, nurses, pharmacies, laboratories and ambulance services. You can find care near you, consult, order medicine, book tests and keep your health record in one place.

Who is behind it?

Afyatel is published by Kas-Annex Medical Center, a private medical clinic in Kasarani, Nairobi, licensed by the Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council. More about us.

Is Afyatel a hospital or a clinic?

No. Afyatel is a technology platform. The providers on it are independent licensed professionals who are responsible for the care they give.

Can I use it in an emergency?

Afyatel is not an emergency service. In an emergency call 999 or 112, or go to the nearest hospital. The Emergency screen is only a shortcut to those numbers and to ambulance operators.

Where is it available?

Afyatel is built for Kenya. Which providers you see depends on who has joined near you.

For patients

How do I see a doctor?

Open Consultation, choose a provider, pick chat, in-clinic or home, choose a time and pay. Watch the steps.

Can I have a video call?

Not yet. Remote consultations are by secure in-app chat for now. Video consultations are planned.

How do I know a provider is genuine?

Every provider gives a licence number at sign-up, which is checked against their regulator before they can see patients. Verified providers carry a tick.

Can I get care for my child?

Afyatel accounts are for people aged 18 and over. A parent or guardian can seek care for a child through their own account.

How do I get my medicine?

After a consultation, send the prescription from your notes to a pharmacy and pin where it should be delivered. Watch the steps.

How do I get lab results?

The laboratory sends them to the app as a note, a photo or a PDF. You are notified, and the doctor who ordered the test sees them too.

Payments

How much does a consultation cost?

Each provider sets their own fee, and you see it before you book. Downloading and creating an account costs nothing.

How do I pay?

With M-Pesa. Afyatel sends a prompt to your phone and you enter your PIN in the M-Pesa prompt. Afyatel never sees your PIN.

Can I use SHA or insurance?

You can ask for a booking or order to be billed to SHA or your insurer by giving the scheme and your member number. The provider bills the scheme and confirms your cover; you remain responsible for anything the scheme does not pay.

Can I pay by card?

Not yet. Card payments are planned.

What if something goes wrong with a payment?

Contact us with the M-Pesa receipt number and we will look into it. Refunds are covered in the Terms of Use.

For providers

Who can join?

Doctors, specialists, clinical officers, nurses, pharmacists, laboratories and imaging providers, and ambulance services. See how it works for providers.

How am I verified?

You give your licence number at sign-up. It is checked against your regulator’s register, and your provider menu unlocks once you are approved.

What does it cost?

There is no joining fee. Afyatel keeps a 15% platform commission on payments made to you through the app.

Do I set my own fees?

Yes. You set your consultation fee, and pharmacies and laboratories set their own prices.

Can I keep my own records?

Yes. Consultation notes, prescriptions and lab orders you write are your clinical record, and you can export a clinical summary as a PDF.

Privacy and your data

Who can see my health record?

You, the providers you book, message or pay, and authorised Afyatel staff. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Do you sell my data or show me targeted ads?

No. We do not sell personal data, and health information is never used to choose advertising.

What permissions does the app ask for?

Location while the app is open, photos and files you pick, and notifications. It does not use your camera or microphone, and does not read your contacts, messages or call logs.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile, then Privacy and legal, then Delete account. If you no longer have the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page.

The pilot build

What is the pilot build?

Afyatel is being tested with invited patients and providers before it is released on Google Play. The pilot build is the Android app used for that testing.

How do I get it?

See Get the app, or ask us for access.

Will I keep my account when the app reaches Google Play?

Your account stays. You will need to install the store version in place of the pilot build.

Is there an iPhone version?

The first release is for Android.
